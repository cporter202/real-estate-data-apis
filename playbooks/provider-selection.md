# Provider selection checklist

Before choosing a real-estate API, compare:

- Geographic coverage and source sites
- Detail fields and historical availability
- Refresh rate, concurrency, and rate limits
- Structured output quality and retry behavior
- API, MCP, CSV, and webhook options
- Pricing at your expected record volume
- Terms of service, privacy controls, and data-retention rules

Shortlist one Apify option and one CoreClaw option, run the same small sample through both, and compare completeness before committing to a production integration.
