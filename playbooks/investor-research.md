# Investor and FSBO research workflow

Combine property-detail extraction with public listing search, location data, and a review step. Keep a source URL and capture timestamp for every record so the output can be audited later.

Recommended fields:

- Address and coordinates
- Asking price and estimated market value
- Property type, beds, baths, and square footage
- Listing status and days on market
- Agent or seller contact fields when publicly provided
- Source URL, retrieval time, and confidence notes

Do not treat an automated estimate as an appraisal or investment advice.
